WamuTailor Policies

1. Orders & Measurements

  • Custom orders are made using the measurements and design details confirmed with the client.
  • Clients should check and confirm style, fabric, colour, design details and measurements before production begins.
  • Clients should notify WamuTailor promptly if their measurements or design requirements change.

2. Deposits & Payments

  • Any required deposit and payment schedule will be agreed and communicated before work begins.
  • The remaining balance is payable according to the agreed order terms, normally before collection or delivery.
  • Always request and keep an official payment confirmation or receipt.

3. Production & Delivery

  • Estimated completion and delivery dates are agreed when the order is placed.
  • Timing may change if the client changes the design, fabric or measurements, or if materials become unavailable; WamuTailor will communicate material changes.
  • Worldwide delivery can be arranged where available; shipping costs, delivery estimates and customs charges are confirmed separately.

4. Fittings, Alterations & Quality

  • Where a fitting is needed, the client should attend at the agreed time.
  • If an item has a workmanship or measurement issue, contact WamuTailor promptly with the order details so the team can assess and agree a reasonable correction.
  • Alterations requested because of a later change in personal preference or body measurements may be charged separately.

5. Cancellations & Refunds

  • Contact WamuTailor as early as possible if you need to cancel or change an order.
  • Because custom garments may involve purchased fabric and work already completed, any refund or cancellation adjustment will be assessed against costs incurred and applicable consumer law.
  • Ready-made items and custom-made items may have different return or exchange conditions. Confirm the applicable terms before purchase.

6. Client Portal & Privacy

  • Clients are responsible for keeping their portal sign-in details private and should change their password if they believe it has been exposed.
  • The portal is intended to show the signed-in client's own orders, measurements and payment history.
  • WamuTailor should use client contact details and measurements only for order handling, customer service and related business purposes, subject to applicable law.

7. Contact & Policy Updates

For questions about an order or these policies, contact WamuTailor through the official shop contact details provided at the time of ordering. These policies may be updated; the terms applicable to a particular order should be confirmed when the order is accepted.

This page is a general customer policy summary, not a substitute for legal advice. Have the final terms reviewed for compliance with Rwanda's applicable consumer and data-protection laws before relying on them as formal legal terms.
